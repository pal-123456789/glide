How it works

Three movements. Nothing to hold.

Move

The webcam reads the pose of your head 30 times a second. A One-Euro filter smooths the signal — steady when you hold still, responsive when you move — so the pointer glides instead of jittering.

Hold

Resting the pointer on a button fills a ring. Complete it and Glide clicks — no physical press required. Prefer eyes to neck? A long, deliberate blink triggers the same click.

Speak

A full keyboard and category boards with word prediction let anyone build a sentence and say it out loud. The prediction adapts to the words you actually use, so common phrases get faster over time.

How it's built

On-device, offline, and honest about it.

No servers, no accounts, no video leaving the room. Everything that watches you runs inside your own browser tab.

Local face tracking

MediaPipe FaceLandmarker runs in-browser via WebAssembly. The video frames are read and discarded on your device — never uploaded, never recorded.

Works offline

Installs as a PWA and caches itself on first load. After that it opens and runs with no internet at all.

Precise by design

A 9-point affine calibration maps your head's range to the screen, and the One-Euro filter keeps the cursor stable at rest.

Reaches everyone

Dwell, blink, head gestures, and single-switch scanning — so it fits a range of abilities, not just one.

Your face never leaves your device.

Assistive tech watches you all day. That data should never become someone else's to hold. Glide is built so there's nothing to hold in the first place — no upload, no account, no analytics pointed at your camera. It's open and inspectable, and it keeps working when the internet doesn't.
